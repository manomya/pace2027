Most important observation about your current website

Your existing design is actually a good starting point. It already has the PACE branding, homepage, countdown, conference information, themes, committees, registration fee table, payment information, venue and other sections.

But currently it is essentially:

Website → Information

You need to turn it into:

Website → Registration → Payment → Paper Submission → Review → Acceptance → Camera Ready → Conference → Certificate

That's the real project.

And because the first deadline in the brochure is 15 September 2026 for abstracts, the registration/submission backend should be treated as a priority rather than building dozens of extra informational pages first.

Phase 1 — Foundation

Convert current HTML into Next.js
